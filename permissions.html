Handsfree Setup

Handsfree runs 100% on-device. Camera access is needed for head tracking, and microphone access is needed for voice recognition.

💡 If Chrome does not show a prompt:
  1. Look at the address bar above: click the Site Settings icon (left of URL).
  2. Set both Camera and Microphone to Allow.
  3. Or visit: chrome://settings/content/all, search for "Handsfree", and allow Camera & Mic.
  4. Reload this page and the side panel.
